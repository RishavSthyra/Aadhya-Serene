                     Aadhya Serene External Lead API



     Endpoint

 1    POST https://www.aadhyaserene.com/api/integrations/leads



     Headers

 1    Authorization: Bearer <shared-secret>
 2    Content-Type: application/json



     Request

 1    {
 2        "leadId": "LEAD-12345",
 3        "name": "Customer Name",
 4        "phone": "9876543210",
 5        "email": "customer@example.com",
 6        "source": "partner_name",
 7        "projectName": "Aadhya Serene",
 8        "enquiryType": "register_interest",
 9        "message": "Please share brochure and pricing.",
10        "submittedAt": "2026-08-03T10:30:00.000Z",
11        "whatsappConsent": true
12    }



     Request Fields

          Field             Required    Notes
          leadId            Yes         Unique and permanent for that lead. Re-send the
                                        same ID on retry.
          name              Yes         Customer name.
          phone             Yes         Indian mobile number. Use 10 digits or country-code
                                        format.
          whatsappConsent   Yes         Must be true.
          source            No          Your platform or campaign name.
